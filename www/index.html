1-Masala | Bor postulatlari Oson

Vodorod atomida elektron 3-pog'onadan (E₃ = -1.51 eV) 2-pog'onaga (E₂ = -3.4 eV) o'tganda nurlanadigan foton energiyasini toping.

Formula: hν = E₃ - E₂ = -1.51 - (-3.4)

Javob: 1.89 eV

2-Masala | De-Broyl to'lqini O'rtacha

Tezligi v = 2·10⁶ m/s bo'lgan elektronning de-Broyl to'lqin uzunligini toping. (mₑ = 9.1·10⁻³¹ kg, h = 6.63·10⁻³⁴ J·s)

Formula: λ = h / (mₑ · v) = 6.63·10⁻³⁴ / (9.1·10⁻³¹ × 2·10⁶)

Javob: 3.64 × 10⁻¹⁰ m (0.364 nm)

3-Masala | Radioaktiv yemirilish Murakkab

Yarim yemirilish davri T = 10 kun. N₀ = 8000 ta atomdan t = 30 kundan keyin qanchasi qoladi?

Formula: N = N₀ · 2^(-t/T) = 8000 · 2⁻³

Javob: 1000 ta

4-Masala | Foton impulsi Oson

To'lqin uzunligi λ = 500 nm bo'lgan yorug'lik kvantining impulsini toping.

Formula: p = h / λ = 6.63·10⁻³⁴ / (500·10⁻⁹)

Javob: 1.33 × 10⁻²⁷ kg·m/s

5-Masala | Fotoeffekt qizil chegarasi O'rtacha

Chiqish ishi A = 2.2 eV bo'lgan metall uchun fotoeffektning qizil chegarasidagi chastotani toping.

Formula: νₖ = A / h = (2.2 × 1.6·10⁻¹⁹) / 6.63·10⁻³⁴

Javob: 5.3 × 10¹⁴ Hz

6-Masala | Massa defekti Murakkab

Geliy yadrosi ⁴₂He ning massa defekti Δm = 0.0304 m.a.b. ga teng. Yadro bog'lanish energiyasini toping.

Formula: E = Δm × 931.5 MeV = 0.0304 × 931.5

Javob: 28.3 MeV

7-Masala | Elektron tezlatgich Oson

U = 10 kV potensiallar farqidan o'tgan elektron qanday kinetik energiyaga ega bo'ladi (keV da)?

Formula: Wₖ = e·U = 10 keV

Javob: 10 keV (1.6 × 10⁻¹⁵ J)

8-Masala | α-yemirilish siljishi O'rtacha

Uran izotopi ²³⁸₉₂U bitta α-yemirilishga uchraganda hosil bo'lgan yangi yadro massasi va zaryadini aniqlang.

Qoida: A kamayadi 4 ga, Z kamayadi 2 ga: ²³⁴₉₀Th (Toriy)

Javob: Massa soni 234, Z = 90

9-Masala | β⁻ yemirilish O'rtacha

Toriy ²³⁴₉₀Th yadro bitta β⁻ yemirilishga uchrasa, qanday element hosil bo'ladi?

Qoida: A o'zgarmaydi, Z bittaga oshadi (Z = 91): ²³⁴₉₁Pa (Protaktiniy)

Javob: ²³⁴₉₁Pa

10-Masala | Rentgen nurlari Murakkab

Rentgen naychasida U = 40 kV kuchlanish berilganda nurlanadigan rentgen nurlarining minimal to'lqin uzunligini toping.

Formula: e·U = h·c / λₘᵢₙ ⇒ λₘᵢₙ = 1240 / U(kV)

Javob: 0.031 nm (31 pm)
